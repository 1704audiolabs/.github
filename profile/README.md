## 1704 Audio Labs — Measure & Taste

**There is no correct sound, only the endless, joyful search for *yours*.**

DIY loudspeakers from affordable parts, measured to understand and voiced to taste. Every plan,
drawing, script and measurement is free to rebuild.

- **[Principles of sound exploration](https://1704audiolabs.github.io/)**: the belief behind everything here
- **01 · [Le Petit](https://1704audiolabs.github.io/le-petit/)**: Jean Hiraga's Le Petit from one sheet of OSB, flat within ±1 dB from 55 to 150 Hz
- **02 · [Le Petit Sub](https://1704audiolabs.github.io/le-petit-sub/)**: its bigger twin, a sealed subwoofer with two opposed 10″ drivers
